Ormer AI is a Jersey-based consultancy and product studio. We design, build, and certify machine-learning systems for clinical use — under EU MDR, UK MDR, FDA, and the EU AI Act. From concept and statistical validation to post-market surveillance.

01
Services

i

EU AI Act × MDR Gap Analysis

End-to-end development of clinical machine-learning models, with statistical analysis plans and validation studies designed for regulatory submission. Current work includes diagnostic ML modelling for respiratory disease in a multi-centre clinical validation study.

Project · Retainer

Project · Audit
02
Approach

Small, senior, and unusually combined.

We are a small, senior team. Engagements are led by founders, not handed to juniors. We work with a limited number of clients at any one time — usually three to five.

Our edge is the rare combination of hands-on machine-learning practice and deep medical-device regulatory experience. Most consultancies have one or the other. We routinely build the model and the technical file.

A clinical co-founder keeps us honest about whether what we build actually works in practice, not just on paper. That perspective shapes every engagement we take on.

CrisisScribe
AI-powered clinical scribing and quality assurance for mental-health crisis teams.

Designed for the most demanding clinical setting we know.

CrisisScribe is Ormer AI's flagship product: an AI-powered clinical scribing and quality-assurance tool, built specifically for mental-health crisis teams. It captures the conversation, structures the clinical record, and surfaces quality-assurance signals to senior clinicians — without adding to the cognitive load of frontline staff.

Currently in pilot with NHS partners. Built on the same regulatory foundations we apply to client work.
