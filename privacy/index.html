Privacy notice

If I have written to you and you did not give me your details, I found them in a public record: a research funding database, a published paper, a company register, or your own website. I hold your name, role, organisation and contact address, and nothing else.

Reply with “no thanks” to any message and I will delete your details and not contact you again. You do not have to give a reason, and it takes effect immediately.

Who is responsible for your data

Ormer AI Ltd, a company registered in Jersey and trading as Ormer Health, is the data controller. You can reach us at hello@ormer-health.ai, or write to Ormer AI Ltd, Saint Helier, Jersey, Channel Islands.

Because we are based in Jersey and contact people in the United Kingdom and the European Union, our handling of personal data is governed by the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 and, where it applies, the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation.

If we contacted you first

Most people reading this page will have arrived from an email or message we sent. The law requires us to tell you what we hold and where it came from, so this is that.

What we hold

  • Your name and professional role
  • The organisation you work for
  • A business email address or professional social media profile
  • Publicly available information about your organisation's work, such as a grant award reference or a published paper

We do not hold your personal address, telephone number, date of birth, financial details, or any special category data such as health, ethnicity, religion or political opinion. We do not build behavioural profiles and we do not buy contact lists.

Where it came from

  • UKRI Gateway to Research and other public research funding registers
  • Published academic papers, including corresponding author details printed in the paper
  • Companies House and equivalent public company registers
  • Your organisation's own website, including team and contact pages
  • Public professional profiles such as LinkedIn
  • Clinical trial registries and conference programmes

If you would like to know precisely which source your details came from, ask and we will tell you.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use it to contact you once, and at most twice, about medical device regulatory services and to offer you a guide we have written. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK and EU GDPR and the corresponding provision of the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018.

The legitimate interest is a small consultancy identifying organisations that may need specialist regulatory help. We have weighed that against your interests and concluded it is reasonable because the data is limited to professional contact details, it comes from sources published for the purpose of professional contact, the message concerns your professional responsibilities rather than your private life, the volume is low and each message is written individually, and you can stop it permanently with a two-word reply. If you disagree with that assessment, tell us and we will stop.

If you contacted us

If you email us, book a call, or download something and choose to tell us who you are, we hold what you send us in order to reply and to carry out any work we agree. Where that becomes a paid engagement, our basis is the performance of a contract; before that point it remains legitimate interests in responding to an enquiry.

The guide, and this website

The guide on this site downloads directly. There is no form, no email address is required, and we do not know who has downloaded it.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Two things happen nonetheless, and you should know about them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security and abuse prevention under its own privacy statement. The site also loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google receives your IP address in the process.

Who else sees your data

Our email is provided by a mainstream business email provider acting as our processor under contract. Beyond that, we do not share your personal data with anyone. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to advertisers, data brokers or other consultants.

We may disclose information where we are legally required to, for example in response to a lawful request from a regulator or court.

Where it is held, and for how long

Your data is held in Jersey and in the systems of our email provider, which may store data in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or the United States under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Jersey holds an adequacy decision from the European Commission and is recognised as adequate by the United Kingdom.

  • If you do not reply: we delete your details within twelve months of first contact
  • If you ask us to stop: we delete your details immediately, keeping only the minimum record needed to make sure we do not contact you again
  • If we work together: we keep records for as long as the engagement runs and afterwards for the period required by professional, tax and regulatory obligations

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Object to our use of your data for direct marketing. This right is absolute; we must stop and we will
  • Be told what we hold about you, and receive a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data erased
  • Restrict how we use it while a question is resolved
  • Object more generally to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Receive your data in a portable format where that right applies

Email hello@ormer-health.ai to exercise any of these. We will respond within one month and there is no charge.

The quickest way to be removed. Reply to any message from us with “no thanks”, or email hello@ormer-health.ai. No explanation is needed and nothing further will be sent.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

  • Jersey: the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner, jerseyoic.org
  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk
  • European Union: the data protection authority in your country of residence

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and change the date at the top. This version is dated 26 August 2026.
